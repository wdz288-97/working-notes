Working notes from a business intelligence analyst

I'm Ding, a business intelligence analyst. Most of my work is answering business questions with data that teams can use in their decisions: owning key KPIs and reviewing them every week and month, explaining what moves them, handling other teams' requests for data, analysis and dashboards, and supporting teams as they plan and carry out new initiatives.

This site records how I do that work day to day. I've tried to be specific, so if you're new to business analysis and something here helps, you're welcome to use it. Next to it sit notes on how I use AI, at work and day to day, and on the books and references I learn from.

It isn't finished, and isn't meant to be. As I gain experience and learn new things, I refine how I work and update these notes. If you see something differently, or would like to exchange ideas, I'd be glad to hear from you.
