Working notes from a business intelligence analyst

I'm Ding, a business intelligence analyst. Most of my work is turning business questions into numbers people can act on: owning key KPIs and reviewing them every week and month, explaining what moves them, answering other teams' data questions, and supporting teams as they plan and carry out new initiatives.

This site is a record of how I actually do that work, written in enough detail that anyone new to business analysis can borrow what's useful. Next to it sit notes on how I use AI, at work and day to day, and on the books and references I learn from.

It isn't finished, and isn't meant to be. I keep adding to it and revising it as my practice changes.
