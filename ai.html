Using AI

How I use AI, at work and day to day

Notes on working with AI tools. The first is about analysis: where AI fits in my workflow, and the two places I check its work. More will follow.

Where AI fits in my analysis

The AI never interprets data I haven't validated, and nothing goes out in its words alone. You can see it at work in the playbook story, where the AI writes the query that splits the drop in orders by channel.

Context is the assetWhen the AI gets something wrong because it lacked context, I add that context to what it reads next time.

On reuseBefore rerunning old work I compare the period, conditions, assumptions and definitions with the new request. A query that was right last quarter isn't automatically right now.

  1. Me and the AIAgree the plan

    Objective, output, tables, joins, filters, grain and calculations. It draws on past SQL, table metadata, metric definitions, business context, team rules and data-privacy standards.

  2. The AIWrite and run the SQL

    Within the plan. Any material change comes back to me for realignment.

  3. First checkpoint, meValidate the raw output

    Columns and filters against the request, tricky computed fields checked, totals reconciled.

  4. The AIExplore and draft

    Exploratory analysis, a first interpretation and charts, each citing its source.

  5. Second checkpoint, meReview the reasoning

    Do the insights follow from the verified output and the business context?

  6. MeFinal interpretation

    The conclusions and visuals are mine. The AI helps rephrase or build slides.

  7. Me and the AIArchive for reuse

    Plan, SQL, checks, output and assumptions saved together, so the work can be rerun.
